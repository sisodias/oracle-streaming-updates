Oracle Streaming for Mac

One app. One button. Live everywhere.

Download for Mac (Apple Silicon)

Version 0.1.0 · 350 MB · macOS 13+ · internal pilot

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Oracle Streaming into Applications.
  2. For the first launch, right-click Oracle Streaming in Applications and choose Open.
  3. If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway, and confirm once.
  4. The app guides you through the rest — permissions, connection, and your accounts.
